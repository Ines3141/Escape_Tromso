Fridtjof Nansen

Scientist • Explorer • Humanitarian

🧊 Greenland Crossing 🚢 Fram Expedition 🕊 Nobel Peace Prize

Nansen was one of Norway's greatest polar explorers and scientist. He studied marine zoology and neuroanatomy. He crossed Greenland on skis 1888, had the Fram Expedition (1893–1896), which are supposed to go the North pole. He purposely froze his specially designed ship, the Fram, into the Arctic ice pack, allowing the natural drift of the ice to carry them toward the North Pole. Though he didn't reach the exact pole, he set a new "furthest north" record. He studied Arctic ice and ocean currents. Nansen helped over 400.000 refugees after World War I and invented the Nansen passport for stateless refugees.

Fun fact: Nansen tried to reach the North Pole by letting his ship drift with the ice.
