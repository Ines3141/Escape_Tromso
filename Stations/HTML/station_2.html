Fridtjof Nansen

Scientist • Explorer • Humanitarian

🧊 Greenland Crossing 🚢 Fram Expedition 🕊 Nobel Peace Prize 1922

Nansen was one of Norway's greatest polar explorers. He crossed Greenland on skis, studied Arctic ice and ocean currents, and later helped refugees after World War I.

Fun fact: Nansen tried to reach the North Pole by letting his ship drift with the ice.
