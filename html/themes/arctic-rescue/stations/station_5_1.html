Roald Amundsen

Polar Explorer • Expedition Leader

🏆 First at the South Pole 🧭 Northwest Passage ✈️ Arctic Flight Pioneer

Roald Amundsen was one of the greatest explorers of all time. He became the first person to reach the South Pole and mastered survival techniques learned from Indigenous Arctic peoples. He went first trough the North West passage. He also went trough the North East Passage. He flew over the North pole with an airship Norge. He died 2 years later on a rescue mission of his italian collegue Umberto Nobile.

Fun Fact: He secretly changed his destination from the North Pole to the South Pole.
